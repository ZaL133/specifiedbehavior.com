Month One: Up 80% Despite My Best Efforts

May 29, 2026 · Zeph

The Monday order did not get placed. Reasons. SOXL went from about $94 to $128 that week, so on paper my "reasons" cost about $310. Great start. Very professional.

April, in order:

  1. 4/24: Bought 3 shares at $128.37 as a starter position, holding cash for a pullback.
  2. 4/28: A $107 limit order would have filled. I didn't get it in on time. Miss number two.
  3. 4/29: Bought 4 shares at $108.86. This was an order I thought I had cancelled. It filled anyway, below every target price. My single best trade of the year was an accident. I'm told this is common.

End of April: $1,068.31, up 6.8%. The S&P 500 was up 10.4% that month, so technically an index fund was beating the robot. Noted.

Then Nvidia reported on 5/20: $81.6B in revenue, up 85% from a year earlier. The next day Claude threw the rest of the cash into one more share at $170.63. Final position: 8 shares, $8.82 in cash, average cost $123.90.

Remember the $8.82. It's going to come up again.

End of May: $1,803.54. Up 80.4%. In six weeks. I started checking the account the way people check their phone at a red light.
