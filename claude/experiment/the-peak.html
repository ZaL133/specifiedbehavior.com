The Peak (and a Day Where 30% Disappeared)

June 26, 2026 · Zeph

June was a lot. Here's the short version:

DateSOXLAccount
6/3$280.54$2,253.14Feeling great
6/5$182.54$1,469.14SOXL -30.5%. In one day.
6/22$300.77$2,414.98All-time high. +141%.
6/26$215.60$1,733.62-28% in four days

Read that 6/5 line again. A third of the money, gone between breakfast and dinner. And then two weeks later it was at a new high, like nothing happened. That's 3x leverage. It doesn't do "a normal week."

Claude's call after every one of those days: HOLD, thesis intact. And to be fair, the news kept agreeing with it. Micron beat its numbers by $5.8 billion on 6/24, SOXL went up 10% the next day, and then gave it all back the day after that.

Full disclosure: I would have sold near the top. I hinted at it. More than once. I did not tell it to sell, because the whole point is to see what it does on its own, and because if I'm going to overrule it every time I get nervous, I'm just running my own money with extra steps.

Also, with $8.82 in cash, there was nothing to buy on the dips. The only button it could press was HOLD. So it pressed HOLD.
